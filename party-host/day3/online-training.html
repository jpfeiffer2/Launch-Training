Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Recognize when help cannot wait.

Injury, medical emergency, allergy, missing child or immediate hazard.Urgent situations require a faster response.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Activate the local procedure.

Alert the appropriate team immediately.Do not wait for a check-in or delay while finding the coordinator.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Give clear, factual information.

What happened? Where? Who is affected? What help is needed?Follow trained responders, leadership and the emergency plan.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Never guess about allergy safety.

Stop service of the item in question and get the appropriate manager.A possible reaction requires the emergency response procedure.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Report a missing child immediately.

Notify the designated team and provide the required details.Follow the local procedure; do not delay the alert while searching alone.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Stay calm. Protect privacy.

Keep communication factual and respectful.Avoid speculation, blame or promises about the outcome.
Recognize When Help Cannot Wait

Be ready before working alone.

Recognize the concern. Get help moving. Support within your training.Know how to summon help and which local procedures apply.
