Trainer Hands-On Checklist

Use this checklist after the online module and during the trainee's supervised shift. Day 1 reinforces the food-safety foundation that supports recipe and service training on Days 2 and 3.

Trainer: demonstrate and coach each task, then observe the trainee perform it. Do not initial an item based only on a verbal answer. Use your park's approved local procedure wherever the checklist identifies a local standard.
Launch Culture & Shift Readiness Foundation
Connects the Launch vision—Create Awesome Memories—to a Krave shift by preparing food safely, following standards, supporting teammates, and caring for guests.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates Launch Core Values by following the approved standard, communicating concerns honestly, accepting coaching, and helping teammates safely.
Trainer
Trainee
Arrives shift-ready in the approved Krave uniform with required hair restraint, personal hygiene, non-slip closed-toe shoes, and park-required protective equipment.
Trainer
Trainee
Reports illness, symptoms, cuts, sores, or other conditions to the Manager on Duty before handling food and follows the manager's direction.

Handwashing & Task Changes Personal Hygiene
Locates the designated handwashing sink and confirms it is accessible and stocked with running water, approved hand soap, and an approved drying supply.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates the complete handwashing process: wet, soap, scrub hands and arms for 20 seconds, rinse, dry, and protect clean hands when turning off a manual faucet.
Trainer
Trainee
Washes hands at required times, including before food preparation, handling clean equipment, or putting on gloves and after contamination risks such as POS, phones, trash, cleaning chemicals, soiled items, personal contact, eating, drinking, or glove removal.
Trainer
Trainee
Completes a trainer-assigned task-change scenario by stopping, washing correctly, resetting the station as needed, and returning with clean hands and new gloves when required.
Trainer
Trainee
Ready-to-Eat Food & Cross-Contamination Control Every Touch
Protects ready-to-eat food from bare-hand contact by using clean single-use gloves, tongs, a spatula, or another approved utensil.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates the glove sequence—wash and dry hands, put on new gloves, use them for one task, discard them when the task changes or they become torn, dirty, or contaminated, then wash before re-gloving.
Trainer
Trainee
Prepares fresh produce using the approved process: inspect, rinse under cool running water, dry completely, and use clean, sanitized tools and the approved green cutting board—never soap or chemical cleaners.
Trainer
Trainee
Recognizes realistic Krave contamination risks—including unwashed produce, hands or gloves, POS, phones, trash, cleaning tools, personal contact, and unclean food-contact equipment—and completes the correct stop, clean, sanitize, wash, and re-glove response.
Trainer
Trainee
Time & Temperature Control Verify—Do Not Guess
Locates the required thermometers and demonstrates how to clean and sanitize a probe thermometer before and after use.
Trainer
Trainee
Checks and records actual equipment and product temperatures using the correct thermometer and location rather than relying on appearance, a timer, or an outside display.
Trainer
Trainee
Applies the Day 1 standards: cold-held TCS food at 41°F or below, hot-held TCS food at 135°F or above, frozen products kept frozen, and chicken tenders verified at 165°F before serving.
Trainer
Trainee
Stops and notifies the Manager on Duty about an unsafe or questionable result, records the issue, and follows the approved corrective-action and disposal procedure without guessing or quietly returning the product to storage.
Trainer
Trainee
Labels, Shelf Life & FIFO Control the Clock
Completes a product label with the product name; opened, thawed, or prepared date and time; use-or-discard date and time; and employee initials before storage.
Trainer
Trainee
Uses the approved recipe or shelf-life guide to calculate the exact use-or-discard date and time from the recorded opened, thawed, or prepared timestamp. Understands that 168 hours is only the general maximum, a shorter product-specific standard controls, and the earliest date and time controls when ingredients are combined.
Trainer
Trainee
Rotates product using FIFO with the earliest use-or-discard date and time positioned for first use; keeps labels visible and never tops off or combines differently dated batches.
Trainer
Trainee
Identifies missing, incomplete, expired, damaged, or questionable product; removes it from use, tells a manager, and follows the park's approved waste-recording and disposal procedure.
Trainer
Trainee
Opening Line Check Inspect Before Service
Reviews today's expected business—including scheduled parties, events, groups, and large food orders—so the team can prepare the correct food and supplies.
Trainer
Trainee
Completes the required equipment and actual product temperature checks and records the item or unit checked with the temperature actually found.
Trainer
Trainee
Physically verifies sanitation, product readiness, approved recipes, cooking equipment, printer or KDS, smallwares, serving supplies, uniforms and protective equipment, food-contact surfaces, and floors.
Trainer
Trainee
Records corrective actions and selects the final opening status that accurately reflects what was found; does not mark an item as passing without checking and involves the Manager on Duty when needed.
Trainer
Trainee
Cleaning, Sanitizing & Local Support Procedures Reset the Station
Locates the park's approved cleaning supplies and chemicals and confirms they are labeled, secured, and physically separated from food and food-contact items.
Trainer
Trainee
Locates clean towels, mops, brooms, and dustpans and identifies which tools are approved for each cleaning task.
Trainer
Trainee
Identifies the park's designated location or process for used towels, dirty mop water, and other soiled cleaning materials without contaminating food-preparation or handwashing areas.
Trainer
Trainee
Properly prepares the three-compartment sink: cleans the sink and drainboards, removes or pre-rinses food soil, and sets separate basins for washing, rinsing, and sanitizing according to posted and product instructions.
Trainer
Trainee
Uses only the approved sanitizer and correct test strip, verifies the solution is within the required range for that product, and corrects or reports a failed result instead of guessing or adding extra chemical.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates the full warewashing sequence—wash, rinse, sanitize for the required contact time, then air-dry completely on the clean drainboard or rack; never towel-dries sanitized items.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates the park's approved food-contact surface and wiping-cloth procedure, including cleaning before sanitizing and storing wiping cloths correctly when applicable.
Trainer
Trainee
Demonstrates the park's approved local trash-handling procedure, including the disposal location, safe response to leaks or spills, and required handwashing, glove change, and station reset before returning to food work.
Trainer
Trainee
Dry Storage Protect It Before Prep
Maintains dry storage clean, dry, well ventilated, protected from heat, moisture, and direct sunlight, and generally between 50°F and 70°F.
Trainer
Trainee
Stores food at least six inches off the floor and at least eighteen inches from exterior walls—never directly on the floor, even temporarily.
Trainer
Trainee
Keeps containers closed, labels and dates visible, chemicals physically separated from food, and products arranged for FIFO rotation.
Trainer
Trainee
Inspects for torn, leaking, damaged, expired, or questionable products and for pest, moisture, odor, shelving, or building concerns; moves questionable product to the designated area and reports concerns immediately.
